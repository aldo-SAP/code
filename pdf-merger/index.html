PDF Merger

All tools

Add PDFs and photos, set the order, and save one combined file. Photos added together share a page, up to 4 per page. Merging happens on this device. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

    Drag a row by its number to change the order. For a PDF, type the pages to keep, like 1,2,4,7-10.

    Add at least one PDF or photo to begin.

    Password-protected PDFs cannot be merged. Remove the password first. Photos in HEIC format may not open in every browser; JPG and PNG always work.
