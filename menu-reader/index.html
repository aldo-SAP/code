Menu Reader

Take or choose a photo of a menu. The page reads it, finds a picture for each dish and shows the menu as cards you can filter.

Keys and privacy

Without keys, pictures come from Wikipedia and each card has a “More photos” link that opens Google Images. The photo stays on this device in the default mode. In Claude mode the photo is sent to Anthropic. Dish names are sent to Wikipedia or Google to find pictures. Keys are kept only in this browser and are visible to anyone who can use it, so use a key with a spending limit.
